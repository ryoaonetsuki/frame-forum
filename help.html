Community guidelines

1. Be kind and respectful — no harassment, hate or personal attacks.
2. No spam, ads or misleading links.
3. Mark outdated products as Closed so buyers are not misled.
4. Keep discussions in the right forum.
5. Report problems instead of engaging with them.

FAQ

Create a post, switch to the Product tab, add your price, buy link, images and screenshots.

Authors mark a post Closed when a shared app or product stops working. Check the badge before buying.

Stay active, be helpful, and the team will notice. Admins can promote members.
